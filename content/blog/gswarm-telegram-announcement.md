## What's New: Telegram Notifications

GSwarm now includes a built-in Telegram monitoring service that keeps you informed about your blockchain activity in real-time. Whether you're on the go, away from your computer, or just want to stay updated without constantly monitoring, this feature has you covered.

The service monitors:
- 🗳️ **Vote changes** - Track your voting activity and increases
- 💰 **Reward updates** - Monitor your accumulated rewards
- 💎 **Balance changes** - Keep tabs on your wallet balance
- 📊 **Progress detection** - Get notified when activity is detected

---

## Why Telegram Monitoring?

**Real-time awareness**: Get notified immediately when your blockchain activity changes, not when you remember to check.

**Mobile-friendly**: Access your updates anywhere, anytime through the Telegram app on your phone, tablet, or desktop.

**Smart notifications**: Only receive alerts when values actually change, preventing notification fatigue.

**Secure setup**: Your bot token, chat ID, and EOA address are stored locally, keeping your setup private and secure.

**Easy integration**: Works alongside your existing GSwarm supervisor—no additional processes or complex setup required.

**Run from anywhere**: Use `gswarm --telegram` from any directory—no need to be in a specific project folder.

---

## Getting Started

Setting up Telegram monitoring is straightforward:

1. **Get your EOA address** from your [Gensyn dashboard](https://dashboard.gensyn.ai)
2. **Create a Telegram bot** with @BotFather
3. **Get your chat ID** by sending a message to your bot
4. **Run GSwarm with the `--telegram` flag** from anywhere

The service will guide you through the initial setup and then start monitoring your blockchain activity automatically.
