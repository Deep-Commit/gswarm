We are thrilled to announce the launch of the new **GSwarm Leaderboard**! This has been a highly requested feature, and we're excited to bring it to our community. The leaderboard introduces a competitive element to GSwarm, allowing you to track your progress, compare your performance with others, and earn bragging rights.

---

## What is the Leaderboard?

The GSwarm Leaderboard is a public ranking of all participating peers, based on the number of wins they have accumulated. It's a great way to see how you're doing and to motivate yourself to keep contributing to the network.

Here's a look at the new leaderboard:

![GSwarm Leaderboard](/leaderboard.png)

The leaderboard displays the following information:

- 🏆 **Rank**: Your position on the leaderboard.
-  peer **Peer ID**: Your unique identifier.
- 💰 **Wins**: The total number of wins you've accumulated.

---

## Key Features

**Real-time updates**: The leaderboard is updated hourly, so you can see your rank change as you accumulate more wins.

**Top 10**: See the top 10 peers and how you stack up against them.

**Search**: You can search for your own EOA address to see your rank and wins.

---

## How to Get on the Leaderboard

To get on the leaderboard, you just need to run GSwarm and start accumulating wins. The more you contribute, the higher you'll climb. It's that simple!

---

## What's Next

This is just the first version of the leaderboard. We have many more features planned, including:

-   **Historical data**: See how your rank has changed over time.
-   **More stats**: We'll be adding more stats to the leaderboard, such as rewards earned.
-   **Leagues**: We're exploring the idea of adding leagues to the leaderboard, so you can compete against peers of a similar level.

We're always looking for feedback, so if you have any ideas for how we can improve the leaderboard, please let us know!

---

## Start Climbing Today!

The leaderboard is live now. Head over to the GSwarm dashboard to check it out and see where you rank. Good luck, and may the best peer win!
